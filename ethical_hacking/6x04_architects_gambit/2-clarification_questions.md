Does Nordstrøm expect a single governing law clause in the SoW, or subsidiary-level legal annexes?

Informs: SoW governing law structure, dispute resolution clauses, and whether local legal counsel is required before signing.
Are there existing contractual relationships, NDAs, or data-residency obligations at subsidiary level that would constrain where findings are stored, who reviews them, or how they are transmitted?

Informs: data handling requirements in the SoW and whether cross-border finding consolidation is legally permissible under current subsidiary agreements.
