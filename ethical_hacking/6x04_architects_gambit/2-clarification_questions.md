# Clarification Questions, Nordstrøm Power Group

## Scope

1. The RFP references a group-wide health check across five subsidiaries. Does Nordstrøm expect parallel assessments of each entity, or a consolidated review prioritised by risk and NIS2 classification?
   _Informs: whether the engagement is structured as five parallel workstreams or one phased group-level assessment — this determines staffing model, timeline, and base pricing._

2. The Norwegian offshore gas and Dutch gas and hydrogen infrastructure assets likely include operational technology. Are OT networks, SCADA systems, or industrial control environments in scope, and if so, to what methodology depth?
   _Informs: whether PTES must be supplemented with ATT&CK for ICS, whether OT-specialist staffing is required, and whether those environments are actively tested or only inventoried._

3. The German solar subsidiary was recently acquired and integration is described as in progress. Is Vanguard expected to assess it against Nordstrøm Group standards, its pre-acquisition baseline, or both — and has the prior competitor audit been formally closed and shared?
   _Informs: German subsidiary scoping, handling of prior audit findings, and whether a conflict-of-interest disclosure is required before design proceeds._

## Timeline

4. Your board presentation is in ninety days. What is the latest date you can receive a consolidated technical report that still allows internal review and narrative integration before that deadline?
   _Informs: whether a full multi-subsidiary engagement is arithmetically feasible or whether phasing is mandatory, with only Phase 1 findings feeding the board plan._

5. Are interim deliverables — a preliminary risk picture or executive summary of early findings — acceptable before the final report, or does the board require a single complete document?
   _Informs: whether delivery can be staged to protect the board deadline, or whether the full scope must compress into one delivery window._

## Budget

6. The retainer signal is 800,000 EUR. Is this a ceiling, a target, or an indicative figure that flexes with confirmed scope — and does it cover all five subsidiaries or only the initial engagement phase?
   _Informs: whether the engagement can be designed to full group coverage or must be value-ranked and capped at a subset of subsidiaries and phases._

## Constraints

7. Each subsidiary operates under a different national NIS2 transposition. Does Nordstrøm expect a single governing law clause in the SoW, or subsidiary-level legal annexes reflecting each jurisdiction?
   _Informs: SoW governing law structure, dispute resolution clauses, and whether local legal counsel is required before signing._

8. Are there existing contractual relationships, NDAs, or data-residency obligations at subsidiary level that would constrain where findings are stored, who reviews them, or how they are transmitted across borders?
   _Informs: data handling requirements in the SoW and whether cross-border finding consolidation is legally permissible under current subsidiary agreements._
