# Clarification Questions, Nordstrøm Power Group

## Scope
1. Should the engagement cover all five subsidiaries in parallel, or is the board expecting a prioritized wave — for example, the two largest Nordic generation assets first, with the remaining subsidiaries phased in afterward?
   _Informs: whether the project plan is built as a single simultaneous assessment or as a staged rollout with distinct sprints and staffing per wave._

2. For the recently acquired German solar subsidiary, is the current IT/security environment still operating on its pre-acquisition architecture, or has it already been migrated onto Nordstrøm's group platform?
   _Informs: whether Germany is scoped as an independent, ground-up assessment or is folded into the group-wide methodology and tooling._

3. Does "cybersecurity posture" in the RFP include the OT/ICS environments at the generation and grid-connected sites, or is the engagement limited to corporate IT and enterprise systems?
   _Informs: whether the team includes ICS-qualified assessors and whether OT-safe testing methodology (no active exploitation on live control systems) is built into the SoW._

## Timeline
4. The board's 90-day plan implies a hard external deadline — does the board expect a single final report at day 90, or interim deliverables (e.g., a preliminary risk summary at day 30-45) that can be presented before the assessment is fully complete?
   _Informs: whether the workplan is structured around one long delivery cycle or checkpointed milestones with intermediate reporting artifacts._

5. Is the 90-day board deadline fixed regardless of scope, or would the board accept a phased delivery where full coverage of all five subsidiaries extends beyond 90 days while a defensible interim position is delivered on time?
   _Informs: whether subsidiary count and depth are held constant with a compressed timeline (higher staffing) or whether scope is deliberately reduced/deferred to protect delivery quality._

## Budget
6. Is the budget structured as a fixed-fee engagement for a defined scope, or a time-and-materials/retainer model that can flex if the German subsidiary or OT scope expands mid-engagement?
   _Informs: how contingency and change-order language is written into the SoW, and whether OT/Germany work is priced as in-scope or as an optional add-on._

7. Is there a separate budget line for any specialist subcontracting (e.g., OT/ICS testing partners, or German-market legal/regulatory advisors), or must all work be delivered from the core team's existing capabilities?
   _Informs: staffing plan and whether the proposal includes third-party subcontractor costs or is built entirely on in-house resourcing._

## Constraints
8. Was a prior security audit or due-diligence assessment performed on the German subsidiary as part of the acquisition, and if so, can that evidence be relied upon, or does it need independent re-validation given the incomplete integration?
   _Informs: whether Germany's findings section is built on independent testing evidence or partially inherits prior-audit evidence, and how any conflict-of-interest or evidence-reliability caveats are documented in the report._

9. Are there any regulatory reporting obligations (e.g., NIS2 competent-authority notifications) tied to this assessment that would require findings to be shared with regulators in specific jurisdictions before or alongside board reporting?
   _Informs: how findings are sequenced, sanitized, or legally reviewed per jurisdiction before distribution, and whether German findings need separate handling from the rest of the group._

## Board expectations
10. Is the board's primary driver regulatory compliance (NIS2 readiness), investor/M&A due diligence, or an actual incident-driven concern — and does the board expect a compliance gap analysis, a technical risk register, or both?
    _Informs: report structure and whether deliverables are framed around a compliance-mapping section versus a pure technical-risk-and-remediation format._

11. Will the final report be presented directly to the board, or does it first pass through a CISO/executive layer that will translate technical findings into a board-level summary?
    _Informs: whether the deliverable set includes a separate board-level executive summary distinct from the technical report, and how much technical detail is retained versus abstracted._

## Technical preferences
12. Does Nordstrøm have a preferred assessment methodology or framework already in use group-wide (e.g., a specific standard for the IT assessment and a separate one for OT), or should the proposal define the methodology from scratch?
    _Informs: whether the proposal cites and aligns to an existing internal framework (faster buy-in, less methodology negotiation) or proposes a new baseline methodology that must be justified and agreed before testing begins._
