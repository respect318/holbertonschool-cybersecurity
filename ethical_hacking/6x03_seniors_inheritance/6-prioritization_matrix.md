## Notes for the audit committee

- **Priority reflects compounded risk, not just the highest individual CVSS number.** F-05 and F-06 both carry an 8.3 High environmental score but are scheduled P2 because their remediation is narrow and doesn't gate other work, whereas F-03/F-09 and F-01/F-10 are P1 largely because they are the load-bearing steps in Chain 1 — fixing them collapses the chain, not just the individual finding.
- **Chain 2 is the clearest example of why chains matter for planning.** No single finding feeding it would independently reach P1 on severity alone (4.6–8.3, mostly Medium), but the CISO-sponsored program is prioritized P1 because the combined path is a realistic, quiet route to bulk regulated health-data exposure — exactly the kind of risk an audit committee needs visibility into even though it wouldn't surface from a finding-by-finding severity sort.
- **F-04 is intentionally P3/Backlog**, not because the underlying misconfiguration is acceptable, but because the recalibration in Task 3 found a credible, evidenced compensating control (the managed mTLS gateway) that lowers live exploitability; the dependency step (SRE confirmation) is what keeps this from being silently dropped rather than deliberately deprioritized.
- **F-07 is deliberately absent from this matrix.** It was retired in triage as DPO-authorized, controlled functionality; the corresponding action item lives in the report's assurance recommendations, not in the remediation budget being negotiated here.
