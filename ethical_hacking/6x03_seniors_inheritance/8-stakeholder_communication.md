# Stakeholder Communication Mapping

**Client:** MediPath Diagnostics
**Engagement reference:** VS-MPD-26-041
**Purpose:** Define who decides, who needs to know, and in what order, before the final report (Task 10) and the executive summary (Task 9) are drafted. The same weakness needs a different first sentence depending on whether it lands on an engineer's desk, the DPO's desk, or the audit committee's table.

## How to read this mapping

**Primary audience** is the one role that can actually authorize or deliver the corrective action — not the role that is merely interested in the outcome, per the engagement's ownership rule. **Secondary audiences** are the roles who need visibility for oversight, regulatory accountability, execution support, or governance sign-off, drawn across the CEO/DPO/Technical Director/CISO/Board Observer lenses that make up the audit committee. **Sequencing** describes real dependencies in the communication flow — who needs to hear something before someone else does, and why — not just a reading order.

General sequencing principle applied throughout: technical owners are briefed first (to confirm feasibility, cost, and remediation timelines), regulatory/DPO framing follows once scope and remediation posture are known, and the audit committee sees quantified, business-framed items last — feeding directly into the Task 9 executive summary and the Task 10 final report, in that order.
