Board Security Brief: Emergency Response to "Crimson Tide"
Part 1 - The One-Pager: Board Security Brief
Current Threat Status
The regional healthcare sector is currently facing a targeted, high-velocity ransomware campaign known as "Crimson Tide," which has compromised five hospitals in our region within the last ten days. We have confirmed that MedDefense is currently vulnerable to the group's primary entry vector—a critical remote code execution flaw in our perimeter firewall—placing us directly within the immediate "blast radius" of this campaign.

Security Posture Verdict
Our current security posture is "Fragile" and "Exposed," characterized by a flat network architecture that provides zero internal resistance once the perimeter is breached. Because we currently store patient databases and backups in plaintext without internal segmentation, any single point of entry will inevitably result in a total loss of both data confidentiality and system availability.

Emergency Response Summary
We are initiating an immediate 72-hour emergency surge to neutralize the "Crimson Tide" playbook before they transition to our network. This plan focuses on "closing the front door" by patching our FortiGate firewall, "saving the lifeboats" by physically air-gapping our backup storage, and "locking the internal gates" by hardening our Active Directory authentication ciphers. These are tactical, high-impact moves designed to ensure that even if an intrusion is attempted, our core medical operations remain resilient and our data remains unrecoverable for the attacker.

Investment Summary
Our initial $120,000 security investment has successfully mapped these critical cracks, but we have reached a point where administrative friction is our greatest risk. For a mere $2,400 in support renewal—less than the cost of a single day of downtime—we can mitigate a localized annual risk that has now escalated to $3.0 million. We are requesting an additional $15,000 emergency contingency fund to bypass standard procurement delays for advanced endpoint protection and to finalize the encryption of our most sensitive patient databases.

Recommendation
I recommend the immediate approval of the $2,400 support renewal and the $15,000 emergency contingency fund to execute the 72-hour mitigation plan. Transitioning to this "Active Defense" posture tonight is the only statistically viable path to ensuring MedDefense remains operational through the coming week.
