ncident Classification Log (CIA Triad)
Incident A (January 15 - Ransomware on Billing Server)
Primary Pillar Impacted: Availability

Justification: The ransomware encrypted the billing server, rendering the system and its critical data completely inaccessible to the finance team for four days.

Secondary Pillar Impacted: Integrity

Connection: The original data and system files were modified (encrypted) by an unauthorized ransomware payload, compromising their original and trustworthy state.

Incident B (February 2 - Patient Portal Broken Access Control)
Primary Pillar Impacted: Confidentiality

Justification: A broken access control allowed unauthorized users (patients) to view sensitive Protected Health Information (PHI) belonging to other patients by simply modifying a URL parameter.

Secondary Pillar Impacted: None

Incident C (March 18 - Pharmacy System Dosage Error)
Primary Pillar Impacted: Integrity

Justification: A database update script bug unintentionally altered the medication dosage values without authorization, resulting in untrustworthy and incorrect medical data across all sites.

Secondary Pillar Impacted: None

Incident D (April 5 - Website Defacement)
Primary Pillar Impacted: Integrity

Justification: The public-facing website was defaced, meaning its content was intentionally modified and replaced by an unauthorized actor.

Secondary Pillar Impacted: Availability

Connection: The legitimate website and its intended informational services were inaccessible to the public for the two hours it took to restore from a backup.

Incident E (May 22 - EHR System Outage)
Primary Pillar Impacted: Availability

Justification: A failed database migration caused a 9-hour operational outage, making the critical EHR system and patient data inaccessible to physicians when it was needed for clinical care.

Secondary Pillar Impacted: None

Incident F (June 10 - Intern's Torrent Client on Internal Network)
Primary Pillar Impacted: Confidentiality

Justification: An unauthorized personal device running a file-sharing (torrent) client was on the internal network for 3 weeks, creating a severe risk of unauthorized access to or exposure of the HR file share.

Secondary Pillar Impacted: None
