### Data Risk Summary
MedDefense's most significant data protection weakness involves **Patient Medical Records and Diagnostic Imaging Data (PHI) in the "In Transit" and "In Use" states**. Although classified as "Restricted", this highly sensitive data traverses a completely flat, unsegmented network (In Transit), exposing it to interception or lateral movement from any compromised device, such as the unpatched MRI scanner or cryptominer-infected billing server. Furthermore, the administrative culture severely undermines "In Use" protections by utilizing shared radiology accounts and explicitly encouraging clinical staff to leave workstations logged in and unattended in public areas. This combination means that despite database-level security, the actual handling of PHI at the endpoints and across the wire is dangerously unprotected and in direct violation of basic healthcare compliance standards.
