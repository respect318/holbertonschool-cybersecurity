# MedDefense Health Systems: Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

## 1. Purpose and Scope
The purpose of this Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is to outline the acceptable use of computer equipment, networks, and data at MedDefense Health Systems. These rules protect patient safety, secure Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI), and ensure MedDefense complies with healthcare regulations (e.g., HIPAA). 

This policy applies to all MedDefense employees, contractors, temporary staff, medical practitioners, and third-party vendors who access MedDefense systems, networks, or data.

## 2. Acceptable Use of Systems
MedDefense information systems, clinical endpoints, and network resources are provided for authorized business and clinical purposes. Users are expected to use these tools professionally, ethically, and lawfully. Occasional, brief personal use of internet and email is permitted, provided it does not interfere with clinical duties, consume excessive network resources, or violate the prohibited activities outlined below.

## 3. Prohibited Activities
To maintain the security of our hospital network, the following activities are strictly prohibited:
* **Bypassing Security Controls:** Attempting to disable, bypass, or alter Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR) software, firewalls, or web filtering tools.
* **Unauthorized Software:** Downloading or installing unapproved applications. The use of unsupported, legacy, or "end-of-life" medical software must be formally isolated and explicitly approved by IT.
* **Account Sharing:** Sharing your personal credentials or using another employee's account to access systems, including shared generic login accounts.
* **Unauthorized Hardware:** Connecting unapproved enterprise assets, such as consumer-grade routers, personal network switches, or unvetted medical IoT devices, to the internal corporate network.

## 4. Personal Devices and Removable Media
* **Removable Media (USB Drives):** The use of personal or unencrypted USB flash drives, external hard drives, or other removable media to transfer or store MedDefense data is strictly prohibited. Only IT-issued, hardware-encrypted USB drives may be used for authorized business purposes.
* **Shadow IT and Personal Devices:** Personal laptops, tablets, and smartphones must not be connected to the internal clinical or administrative networks. Personal devices may only connect to the designated "Guest" Wi-Fi network. Conducting MedDefense business on unauthorized personal cloud storage accounts (e.g., personal Google Drive or Dropbox) is forbidden.

## 5. Password and Authentication Requirements
* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** MFA is mandatory for all external remote access (including the FortiGate VPN) and for all internal accounts with administrative privileges. 
* **Session Locking:** Users must manually lock their clinical workstations (Windows Key + L) whenever they step away from their desks or medical carts. All endpoints are configured to automatically lock after a brief period of inactivity.

## 6. Data Handling
All MedDefense data must be handled according to its official classification:
* **Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI) & Financial Data:** Must only be accessed by authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis. This data must never be saved to local, unencrypted hard drives, mobile medical workstations, or unapproved third-party applications.
* **Encryption:** All company-issued laptops and mobile workstations are equipped with Full-Disk Encryption. Users must not attempt to decrypt or alter these protections.

## 7. Monitoring and Enforcement
* **No Expectation of Privacy:** MedDefense reserves the right to audit, log, and monitor all activities on its networks and company-owned endpoints (including email, web traffic, and application usage) to ensure compliance and network security.
* **Enforcement:** Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy compromise patient safety and organizational security. Non-compliance will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, and potentially legal or regulatory penalties.
