Why the evidence does not carry it: three distinct facts are proven, and none of them is transfer.
  1. A C2 channel existed and was live at capture (netscan 198.51.100.47:443 ESTABLISHED, PID 1676) — this proves capability, not that bytes moved over it.

The specific evidence that would have been required to prove exfiltration, and was never collected:
  - a perimeter egress record with byte counts (proxy / firewall / NetFlow), or
  - a completed-transfer artefact on the host (a BITS transfer job, an upload log, a sent-bytes record).
ASHFORD-PARA-05 is a workstation; no such egress record exists in either image, and the memory capture did not include one. Therefore exfiltration is not establishable from this evidence.
Proof tiers: access + staging + live channel = proven; exfiltration = declined (not supported, not inferred — the record that would establish it was never in scope).
Bounded disclosure sentence: "Privileged client documents were accessed and gathered into an archive on the workstation, and an active command channel was present; the available evidence does not establish that any of that data was transmitted off the network."
