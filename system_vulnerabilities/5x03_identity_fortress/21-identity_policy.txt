1. PASSWORD POLICY
------------------
To mitigate credential-based attacks, the following rules apply:
- Complexity: Minimum 14 characters. Must include uppercase, lowercase, 
  numbers, and special characters (!@#$%^&*).
- History: Users cannot reuse any of their last 10 passwords.
- Aging: Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
- Storage: Passwords must be hashed using SHA-512 or Argon2.

2. ACCOUNT LOCKOUT POLICY
-------------------------
To prevent brute-force and dictionary attacks:
- Threshold: Accounts will be locked after 5 consecutive failed attempts.
- Duration: Initial lockout period is 30 minutes.
- Reset: After the lockout period, the counter resets. Repeated lockouts 
  (3 within 24h) require manual administrator intervention.

3. SSH ACCESS POLICY
--------------------
- Authentication: Password-based authentication is STRICTLY PROHIBITED.
- Keys: Only ED25519 or RSA (min 3072-bit) keys are permitted.
- Root Access: Direct 'root' login via SSH is disabled. 
- Timeout: Idle sessions will be terminated after 15 minutes of inactivity.

4. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA) REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------------------------------
- Scope: MFA is mandatory for all SSH access to production environments.
- Method: Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) via Google Authenticator.
- Enforcement: Connection requires BOTH a valid SSH key AND a TOTP code.

5. ACCOUNT LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT
-------------------------------
- Creation: Accounts are created only upon signed HR request. 
  Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP) is applied by default.
- Modification: Any privilege escalation must be approved by a manager 
  and logged in the ticketing system.
- Termination: Accounts must be disabled within 1 hour of employee 
  departure or contract termination.

6. EMERGENCY ACCESS PROCEDURES (SUMMARY)
----------------------------------------
- Break-Glass: Use of offline emergency scratch codes is permitted.
- Authorization: Emergency access requires a documented incident ticket.
- Audit: All emergency sessions must undergo a 24-hour post-mortem 
  review and secret rotation (keys/TOTP secrets).

===============================================================================
Compliance: This policy is subject to quarterly audits. 
Non-compliance may result in immediate revocation of access.
