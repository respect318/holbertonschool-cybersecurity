# CISO Compliance Brief

**Prepared for:** Dr. Morales, CISO — MedDefense Health Systems
**Date:** 2026-04-25
**Purpose:** Board presentation — OCR audit readiness

---

## Current Compliance Posture

**At Risk.** MedDefense is not fully prepared for the pending OCR desk audit. The gap assessment reveals that three foundational governance policies — Acceptable Use, Data Classification, and Vendor Management — did not exist in written, enforceable form until this week. A key cloud vendor storing backup archives that contain electronic protected health information (ePHI) does not yet have a signed Business Associate Agreement. Security awareness training covers only 73 percent of the workforce, below our internal 95 percent target. These are not theoretical risks: they are items an OCR auditor will ask for on day one. The organization has made meaningful progress in the past two weeks, and a focused effort over the next two to three weeks can close the most critical gaps before the audit date.

---

## Top Three Findings Before the Audit

1. **We cannot prove to an auditor that our workforce is bound by documented security rules.** The Acceptable Use Policy, Data Classification Policy, and Vendor Management Policy did not exist in signed, distributable form until this project produced them. Without these documents, MedDefense cannot demonstrate governance over how employees handle patient data or use organizational systems.

2. **A vendor that stores our patient backup data has no signed legal agreement protecting that data.** CloudVault Medical holds ePHI in backup archives. Federal HIPAA rules require a signed Business Associate Agreement before any such vendor may receive patient data. No BAA is currently executed. If the audit occurs before this is remediated, MedDefense faces direct regulatory exposure.

3. **Over one in four employees has not completed required security awareness training, and we have no phishing simulation evidence for the past year.** Only 1,460 of 2,000 workforce members completed training in the reporting period. Contractor completion is at 66 percent. HIPAA 164.308(a)(5) requires documented, role-based training for the entire workforce. The current evidence only partially supports compliance.

---

## What We Have Completed

- **Three governance policies drafted and submitted for approval:** Acceptable Use Policy, Data Classification Policy, and Vendor Management Policy were created from scratch and are ready for CISO signature and distribution, closing the most critical gap identified in the prior policy inventory.
- **Vendor risk assessment completed for CloudVault Medical:** A formal security questionnaire was administered, a subprocessor review was conducted, and a vendor risk assessment was documented — providing the evidentiary foundation required to execute the BAA.
- **Audit evidence package assembled:** Supporting artifacts — including endpoint hardening baseline, incident response playbook index, forensic findings summary, and recovery validation summary — have been organized and indexed for auditor review.
- **Risk register updated with five identified risks:** Each risk is documented with severity, owner, and target remediation date, demonstrating to the auditor that MedDefense has an active risk management process.
