# CISO DR Brief

## Program status
MedDefense's risk posture has significantly stabilized following the successful implementation of the Disaster Recovery program. The organization is now in a validated, tested state and demonstrates full compliance with HIPAA 164.308(a)(7) contingency planning mandates.

## Key BIA finding
The Business Impact Analysis (BIA) indicates that LIS/EHR downtime exceeding 15 minutes presents an unacceptable patient safety risk due to the unavailability of critical lab results needed for urgent clinical diagnostics.

## Recovery test result
We completed a successful LIS database recovery test on 2026-06-06. The actual recovery time was 8 minutes and 33 seconds, successfully meeting our 30-minute RTO.
