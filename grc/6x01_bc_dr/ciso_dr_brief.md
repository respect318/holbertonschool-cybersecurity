# CISO DR Program Status Brief

**To:** Board Risk Committee  
**From:** Dr. Morales, CISO  
**Date:** 2026-06-06

### Program Status
The MedDefense Disaster Recovery program is currently in a "Tested" state. All core clinical systems (LIS/EHR) have formal runbooks and are compliant with HIPAA 164.308(a)(7) requirements for contingency planning.

### Key BIA Finding
The Business Impact Analysis (BIA) identified that LIS downtime beyond 15 minutes poses a high risk to patient safety, specifically regarding the delay of critical lab values (e.g., Troponin, Potassium).

### Recovery Test Result
We successfully executed an LIS recovery test. The actual recovery time was 8 minutes and 33 seconds, well within our declared 30-minute RTO.

### Items Requiring Board Action or Budget
* Allocation of resources for automated DR testing environments.
* Budget for redundant clinical downtime paper kits across all sites.
