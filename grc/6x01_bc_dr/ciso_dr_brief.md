# CISO DR Brief

### Program status
The MedDefense Disaster Recovery program is successfully tested and compliant with regulatory contingency planning standards.

### Key BIA finding
Downtime of the LIS/EHR systems beyond 15 minutes presents a critical patient safety risk due to the unavailability of lab results.

### Recovery test result
We executed an LIS recovery test. The actual recovery time was 8 minutes and 33 seconds, successfully meeting our 30-minute RTO.
