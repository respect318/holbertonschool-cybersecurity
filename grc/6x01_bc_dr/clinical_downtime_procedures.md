# Clinical Downtime Procedures

---

## Epic EHR Downtime Procedures

### Who Declares Downtime

The **Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)** officially declares a hospital-wide Epic EHR downtime. The Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100) notifies all nursing units. Do not wait for an official declaration to begin using paper forms if Epic is clearly unavailable — begin immediately and notify your charge nurse.

### Where to Find Paper Forms

- **ICU:** Red binder labeled "Downtime Forms" at the charge nurse desk. Paper MARs in the locked lower drawer under the charge nurse workstation. Allergy verification cards in the wall pocket next to the medication room door.
- **Emergency Department:** Downtime binder in cabinet 3, top shelf at the ED charge station. Paper triage forms in the triage desk left drawer. Paper MARs in the Pyxis room wall file.
- **Medical-Surgical Unit:** Blue binder rack at the central nursing station. Physician order sheets in the unit clerk desk right cabinet. Paper MARs on the shelf above the barcode scanner docking station in the medication room.

### Steps During Downtime

1. **Notify your charge nurse immediately** that Epic is unavailable.
2. **Retrieve the downtime binder** from your unit's designated location (see above).
3. **Use the Paper MAR** to document all medication administration. Record patient full name, date of birth, MRN, medication, dose, time, and your initials on every entry.
4. **Use the Allergy Verification Card** before administering any medication. Verbally confirm allergies with the patient or family.
5. **Use the Physician Order Sheet** for all new orders. Physicians must sign each order. For verbal orders, complete the Verbal Order Read-Back Form and have the ordering physician confirm.
6. **Use the Clinical Assessment Sheet** to document vital signs and nursing assessments at each scheduled interval.
7. **Do not delay urgent patient care.** If you are unsure about a medication or order and cannot verify it manually, escalate immediately.
8. **Keep all completed paper forms together** and secure. Do not discard any paper documentation.

### Escalation Path

1. Primary nurse → Charge nurse
2. Charge nurse → Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100)
3. Nursing Supervisor → Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)
4. For medication concerns: nurse → charge nurse → Pharmacist on Duty (ext. 7400) → attending physician
5. For Epic status questions: Clinical Applications Lead (ext. 7300) or IT Incident Commander (ext. 7200)

### After Epic Is Restored — Reconciliation

1. **The charge nurse initiates reconciliation** using the Downtime Reconciliation Cover Sheet from the unit clerk desk.
2. **Enter all paper MAR entries into Epic** in the correct time sequence. Do not skip or combine entries.
3. **Enter all physician orders and verbal orders** that were written on paper. Confirm with the ordering physician that each order is complete and accurate.
4. **Enter all clinical assessments and vital signs** recorded during downtime.
5. **The charge nurse signs off** on the Downtime Reconciliation Cover Sheet confirming all entries are complete.
6. **Retain all paper forms** in the patient chart or unit binder per your unit policy. Do not destroy them.

## Laboratory Information System Downtime Procedures

### Who Declares Downtime

The **Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)** declares a hospital-wide LIS downtime. The Clinical Applications Lead (ext. 7300) confirms the LIS status. Lab accessioning staff at extension 7500 notify the laboratory department. Clinical staff should begin manual procedures immediately if the LIS is clearly unavailable without waiting for a formal declaration.

### Where to Find Paper Forms

- **Laboratory (Accessioning Desk):** Yellow binder labeled "LIS Downtime." STAT requisition forms in tray 1. Routine requisition forms in tray 2. Specimen rejection log in tray 3. Manual specimen tracking log at the accessioning desk.
- **Emergency Department:** Lab requisition forms in the second drawer of the specimen collection cart.
- **Critical Value Call Log:** Chemistry bench clipboard in the laboratory.

### Steps During Downtime

1. **Notify your charge nurse and the lab accessioning desk (ext. 7500)** that the LIS is unavailable.
2. **Use STAT Lab Requisition forms** (tray 1 at accessioning or ED specimen cart) for all urgent orders. Use Routine Lab Requisition forms (tray 2) for non-urgent orders.
3. **Complete all required fields on every requisition:** patient full name, MRN, date of birth, collection date and time, collector initials, test requested, and ordering provider. **Missing any identifier will result in specimen rejection.**
4. **Label specimens manually** using the same minimum identifiers listed above. Attach the requisition form securely to the specimen.
5. **Deliver specimens to the accessioning desk directly.** Do not leave specimens unattended.
6. **Lab accessioning staff record each received specimen** in the Manual Specimen Tracking Log.
7. **For STAT results**, the lab technician calls the ordering unit directly. The receiver must perform a **read-back of all critical values** and state their name. The lab tech documents the receiver's name, time of call, and confirmation of read-back on the Critical Value Call Log (chemistry bench clipboard).
8. **Rejected specimens** (unlabeled or mismatched) are recorded in the Specimen Rejection Log at accessioning tray 3. The ordering clinician is notified immediately for recollection.

### Escalation Path

1. STAT order issue: ordering clinician → Lab Accessioning (ext. 7500) → Chemistry Lead (ext. 7510) → Laboratory Medical Director (ext. 7520)
2. Critical value — no answer in 5 minutes: escalate to charge nurse
3. Critical value — no answer in 10 minutes: escalate to Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100)
4. Hospital-wide decision: Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)

### After LIS Is Restored — Reconciliation

1. **Lab accessioning leads reconciliation.** All manual specimen tracking log entries are compared against the LIS queue to confirm every specimen collected during downtime is accounted for.
2. **Enter all manual requisitions into the LIS** in the correct collection time order. Do not batch or combine separate orders.
3. **Confirm all results reported verbally during downtime** are entered into the LIS and linked to the correct patient record.
4. **The Chemistry Lead (ext. 7510) reviews the Critical Value Call Log** to confirm that every critical value called during downtime has a corresponding entry in the LIS with receiver name and time.
5. **Retain all paper requisitions and logs** as part of the permanent record. Do not discard any downtime documentation.
6. **Notify the ordering clinicians** that LIS results are now available electronically and that they should verify their patients' results in the system.
