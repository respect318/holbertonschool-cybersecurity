# Clinical Downtime Procedures

---

## Epic EHR Downtime Procedures

### Who Declares Downtime

The **Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)** officially declares a hospital-wide Epic EHR downtime. The Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100) notifies all nursing units. Do not wait for an official declaration — begin using paper forms immediately if Epic is unavailable and notify your charge nurse.

### Where to Find Paper Forms

- **ICU:** Red binder labeled "Downtime Forms" at the charge nurse desk. Paper MARs in the locked lower drawer under the charge nurse workstation. Allergy verification cards in the wall pocket next to the medication room door.
- **Emergency Department:** Downtime binder in cabinet 3, top shelf at the ED charge station. Paper triage forms in the triage desk left drawer. Paper MARs in the Pyxis room wall file.
- **Medical-Surgical Unit:** Blue binder rack at the central nursing station. Physician order sheets in the unit clerk desk right cabinet. Paper MARs on the shelf above the barcode scanner docking station in the medication room.

### Steps During Downtime

1. **Notify your charge nurse immediately** that Epic is unavailable.
2. **Retrieve the downtime binder** from your unit location listed above.
3. **Use the Paper MAR** to record every medication given. Include patient full name, date of birth, MRN, medication, dose, time, and your initials on each line.
4. **Use the Allergy Verification Card** before giving any medication. Verbally confirm allergies with the patient or family.
5. **Use the Physician Order Sheet** for all new orders. Physicians must sign each entry. For verbal orders, complete the Verbal Order Read-Back Form and have the physician confirm.
6. **Use the Clinical Assessment Sheet** to document vital signs and nursing assessments at every scheduled interval.
7. **Do not delay urgent care.** If you cannot verify a medication or order manually, escalate at once.
8. **Keep all paper forms together and secure.** Do not discard any documentation until reconciliation is complete.

### Escalation Path

1. Primary nurse → Charge nurse
2. Charge nurse → Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100)
3. Nursing Supervisor → Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)
4. Medication concern: nurse → charge nurse → Pharmacist on Duty (ext. 7400) → attending physician
5. Epic status question: Clinical Applications Lead (ext. 7300) or IT Incident Commander (ext. 7200)

### After Restoration — Reconciliation

1. **The charge nurse opens the Downtime Reconciliation Cover Sheet** from the unit clerk desk and leads the reconciliation process.
2. **Enter all Paper MAR entries into Epic** in the correct time order. Do not skip or combine entries.
3. **Enter all physician and verbal orders** written on paper. Confirm accuracy with the ordering physician.
4. **Enter all clinical assessments and vital signs** recorded during downtime.
5. **The charge nurse signs the Reconciliation Cover Sheet** confirming all entries are complete.
6. **Retain all paper forms** in the patient chart or unit binder per unit policy. Do not destroy them.

## Laboratory Information System Downtime Procedures

### Who Declares Downtime

The **Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)** declares a hospital-wide LIS downtime. The Clinical Applications Lead (ext. 7300) confirms LIS status. Lab accessioning staff (ext. 7500) notify the laboratory department. **Do not wait for a formal declaration** — begin manual procedures immediately if the LIS is clearly unavailable and notify your charge nurse.

### Where to Find Paper Forms

- **Laboratory — Accessioning Desk:** Yellow binder labeled "LIS Downtime." STAT requisition forms in tray 1. Routine requisition forms in tray 2. Specimen rejection log in tray 3. Manual specimen tracking log on the accessioning desk.
- **Emergency Department:** Lab requisition forms in the second drawer of the specimen collection cart.
- **Critical Value Call Log:** Chemistry bench clipboard in the laboratory.

### Steps During Downtime

1. **Notify your charge nurse and lab accessioning (ext. 7500)** that the LIS is unavailable.
2. **Use a STAT Lab Requisition form** (tray 1 at accessioning or ED specimen cart) for urgent orders. Use Routine Lab Requisition forms (tray 2) for non-urgent orders.
3. **Complete every required field on the requisition:** patient full name, MRN, date of birth, collection date and time, collector initials, test requested, and ordering provider. **Incomplete labels will result in specimen rejection.**
4. **Label each specimen manually** using the same identifiers. Attach the requisition form securely to the specimen container.
5. **Deliver specimens directly to the accessioning desk.** Do not leave specimens unattended.
6. **Lab accessioning records each specimen** in the Manual Specimen Tracking Log upon receipt.
7. **For critical values,** the lab technician calls the ordering unit directly. The receiver must perform a **read-back** and state their name. The tech documents the receiver name, time, and read-back confirmation on the Critical Value Call Log.
8. **Rejected specimens** are logged in the Specimen Rejection Log (tray 3). Notify the ordering clinician immediately for recollection.

### Escalation Path

1. STAT order issue: ordering clinician → Lab Accessioning (ext. 7500) → Chemistry Lead (ext. 7510) → Laboratory Medical Director (ext. 7520)
2. Critical value — no answer after 5 minutes: escalate to charge nurse
3. Critical value — no answer after 10 minutes: escalate to Nursing Supervisor (ext. 7100)
4. Hospital-wide decision: Administrator on Call (ext. 7000)

### After Restoration — Reconciliation

1. **Lab accessioning leads reconciliation.** Compare the Manual Specimen Tracking Log against the LIS queue to confirm every downtime specimen is accounted for.
2. **Enter all manual requisitions into the LIS** in collection time order. Do not batch separate orders together.
3. **Verify that all verbal results reported during downtime** are entered in the LIS and linked to the correct patient record.
4. **The Chemistry Lead (ext. 7510) reviews the Critical Value Call Log** to confirm each critical value has a complete entry including receiver name and time.
5. **Notify ordering clinicians** that LIS results are now available electronically and ask them to verify their patients' results.
6. **Retain all paper requisitions and logs** as permanent records. Do not discard any downtime documentation.
